# FM-1 Felucca-Tutor 🎹🎓

An interactive, step-by-step tutorial firmware for the **M-VAVE FM-1**, built on top of the open-source **Felucca** framework. 

This firmware transforms the FM-1 into an interactive learning machine: learn synthesis, sequencing, groove design, and pattern chaining with real-time audio feedback.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Interactive Guided Tour ("Tour Mode"):** On-screen guidance step-by-step directly on the 240x240 display.
* **Hands-on Sequencing:** Learn 16-step sequencing, ratchets, and pattern variation on the fly.
* **Song Construction:** Discover pattern chaining to build full tracks from short loops.
* **Sound Design & Psychoacoustics:** Live exploration of filters, envelopes, FM/wavefolding, and spatial effects (Reverb/Delay).
* **Real-time Audio Feedback:** Every step you make is instantly audible.

---

## 🚦 Interactive Tutorial Roadmap

1. **Phase 1: Basics & Sequencer**
   * Notes, drum tracks, 16-step grid creation.
   * Creating Pattern A & Pattern B variations.
   * Pattern Chaining.
2. **Phase 2: Sound Design & Timbre**
   * Filter cutoffs & resonance visualization.
   * Envelopes (Plucks vs. Pads).
   * FM synthesis & wavefolding harmonics.
3. **Phase 3: Rhythm & Dynamics**
   * Ratchets & micro-timing.
   * Euclidean rhythms & probability steps.
4. **Phase 4: Arrangement & Psychoacoustics**
   * Parameter locks & build-up automation.
   * Reverb & Delay spatial placement.
